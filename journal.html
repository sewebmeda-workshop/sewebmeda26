Journal of Biomedical Semantics (JBMS) Submission

Submission Deadline: October 15, 2026

All submitted manuscripts at workshop as well as new ones will be invited for submitting paper at "Journal of Biomedical Semantics" (JBMS). Journal submission must at-least be 30%-40% new and additional content and full article must not be less than 1000 words.

Please visit Journal of Biomedical Semantics for submission Guidelines. Before you submit, take some time for reading and ensuring that the submission matches the guidelines defined by the Journal. Journal follows specific submission format.

Instructions:

  • If Full paper have been accepted at SeWeBMeDA workshop they still undergo a light-weight review.
  • In case of Short/ Demo papers have been accepted at SeWeBMeDA workshop they undergo full / regular review.
  • In case of new submissions they undergo full / regular review.
  • Altogether, the review process will be based on submitted paper at JBMS, NOT the workshop submission/ acceptance/participation. This is necessary to keep up the journal's quality considerations.
  • Submissions at JBMS must be acknowledged (in abstract) as if this research had been the part of the proceedings published at the SeWeBMeDA workshop. Furthermore, authors should send an email to sewebmeda@gmail.com at the time of submission mentioning the Title of the paper, list of all the co-authors and the name of original paper published at the SeWeBMeDA.
  • For all the Journal submission format, requirements and other related enquiries, contact juliet.maloloyon@springernature.com (for submission enquiries) or info@biomedcentral.com (for general enquiries)

Important notice: Journal of Biomedical semantics is a separate entity with distinguished editorial board. Therefore, SeWeBMeDA workshop has no control over timelines, decisions and other related aspects of quality control.
