Conference Venue

Royal Princess Hotel Dubrovnik

Ul. kardinala Stepinca 31a, 20000, Dubrovnik, Croatia

The hotel is located along the beautiful Lapad coastline and offers elegant seaside accommodation, modern conference facilities, and exceptional hospitality all within easy reach of Dubrovnik's historic Old City.

ESWC 2026 participants can benefit from a special discounted conference rate through the official booking link.

Conference Registration

Registration is mandatory for attending the Workshop. At least one author from each accepted paper must attend in person to present their work.

Information about registration fees and important deadlines is available here. Please note that the Early Bird registration rate is valid until March 16.
